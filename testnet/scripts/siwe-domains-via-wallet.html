SIWE domains — SignetGroup

Sets the ERC-4361 domains this group accepts. Two transactions with a timelock between them. Nothing here holds or sees your key; your wallet signs.

The list is replaced wholesale, not appended to. Whatever is queued below becomes the complete set of domains this group will accept — anything currently listed and not repeated here is removed. An empty list disables the onchain_resolver scheme; empty never means "any domain".

Domains are matched exactly. sfluv.org and app.sfluv.org are different entries, as are app.sfluv.org and app.sfluv.org:443. This must match what the application actually puts in the SIWE message it asks users to sign.
